ANAS AI Encrypted Backups

A private backup tool operated by Anas Shalash for his personal ANAS AI assistant. It is independent of NOVA and does not access NOVA accounts or data.

Google Drive access uses only drive.file to create and manage files belonging to this backup app. Backup contents are encrypted before upload; A separate recovery key is required before activating real backups and must be held outside Google Drive.

Current status: personal Drive connection and an encrypted synthetic upload/download/memory restore have been tested. Automated daily backups, real Production restore and 14-day provider retention are not activated or verified yet.

Only the authenticated owner may connect an account. No Google Drive-wide access is requested. No purchase or new subscription is offered.
